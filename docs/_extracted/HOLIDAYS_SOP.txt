1. LEAD MANAGEMENT SOP  
Lead Sources:  
• Meta Ads  
• Website Enquiry

• WhatsApp

Step 1: Lead Response Time  
1. Response Timeline  
• All leads must be responded to within 15 minutes of lead generation or 
capture.  
• This applies to leads from all sources: Meta ads, website, WhatsApp, phone 
calls, walk -ins, email inquiries, or any third -party platforms.  
Purpose:  
• Quick engagement increases the likelihood of lead conversion.  
• Reduces risk of competitor outreach capturing the lead.  
 
2. Mode of Initial Contact  
Step 1 – Call:  
• Call the lead first to establish direct communication.  
• Introduce yourself, your travel agency, and the services relevant to the lead 
(e.g., Schengen/US/UK visas, holiday packages).  
• Keep the conversation concise, professional, and engaging.  
• Identify client eligibility and readiness.  

===== PAGE 2 =====
Step 2 – WhatsApp Follow -Up: 
• If the call is not answered, immediately send a WhatsApp message.  
• Include:  
o Your name & agency name  
o Reference to their inquiry (destination or visa type)  
o A clear CTA: “Please share a convenient time for a call” or “Click here 
to start your application”  
• WhatsApp ensures the lead sees your message even if they miss the call.  
 
3. Lead Status Updates  
• Immediately update CRM / lead tracking sheet after every contact attempt.  
• Track the following statuses:  
1) Contacted – Answered  
2) Contacted – No Answer  
3) Follow -Up Scheduled  
4) Quote Sent  
5) Payment Pending  
6) Not Interested  
7) Closed / Converted  
• Ensure all notes about client eligibility, preferred travel dates, and special 
requests are recorded.  
Purpose:  
• Maintains accurate records for sales reporting, follow -ups, and performance 
metrics.  
• Allows other team members to see lead history and avoid repeated outreach.  
 
4. Escalation & Monitoring (Team Leader / Manager):  
• If a lead is not contacted within 15 minutes, escalate to the Holidays/Travel

5. PAYMENT SOP  
Booking Confirmation Rules:  
1. Minimum 50% Advance Payment  
• A minimum of 50% advance payment is mandatory to initiate booking 
confirmation.  
• Advance payment is required to block and secure hotel rooms, flights, 
transfers, tours, or any land services.  
• No services will be held or blocked beyond supplier time limits without 
receipt of advance payment.  
• The advance amount must reflect in the company account or valid payment 
proof must be shared before processing.  
• Verbal confirmations or email approvals without payment will not be treated 
as confirmed bookings.  
Purpose:  
To secure inventory, minimize cancellation risk, and protect the company from 
supplier penalties.  
 
2. 100% Payment for Non -Refundable Bookings  
• For all non -refundable or instant purchase bookings (e.g., promotional airfares, 
special hotel offers, peak season deals), 100% payment is mandatory upfront.  
• These bookings cannot be cancelled, amended, or refunded once 
issued/confirmed.  
• Clients must be informed clearly in writing about non -refundable terms before 
collecting payment.  
• Written approval from the client is required before issuing non -refundable 
services.  
Purpose:  
To avoid financial liability arising from non -refundable supplier policies.  
 
3. Balance Payment Before D -2 (As Per Cancellation Policy)  
• The balance payment must be cleared before D -2, i.e., 2 days prior to the 
supplier’s final payment deadline.  
• For each service (hotel, land package, or flight), the supplier’s last date of 
payment will be considered the reference for D -2. 

===== PAGE 15 =====
• Failure to pay the balance by this timeline may result in automatic 
cancellation or release of blocked services as per the supplier’s policy.  
• Any cancellation fees or penalties imposed due to delayed balance payment 
will be charged to the client.  
Purpose:  
To ensure bookings are fully secured and to comply with supplier payment terms, 
minimizing the risk of service cancellation or penalties.  
 
4. No Service Confirmation Without Payment Proof  
No booking will be processed, ticketed, or confirmed without valid payment proof.  
Acceptable payment proof includes:  
• Bank transfer confirmation  
• Online payment receipt  
• POS transaction slip  
• Official company payment confirmation  
Screenshot without transaction reference or pending transaction proof will not be 
considered valid confirmation.  
Booking confirmation timeline (ticketing/voucher issuance) begins only after 
payment validation.  
Purpose:  
To maintain financial control, avoid fraudulent claims, and ensure accounting 
compliance.  
 
Compliance Rule  
• Sales team must clearly communicate payment terms at quotation stage.  
• Payment deadlines must be mentioned in writing (email/WhatsApp).  
• CRM must reflect payment status before marking booking as confirmed.  
• Accounts team confirmation is mandatory for large -value bookings.

6. BOOKING CONFIRMATION SOP  
Before Payment (Blocking Stage)  
• Block hotel and land arrangements with DMC for 24 –48 hours (as per supplier 
policy)  
• Block flight seats (if applicable) as per airline fare rules (time -limit based)  
• Share payment deadline with client clearly (mention fare/room release time)  
• Record blocking deadline in CRM  
• Follow up with client before blocking expiry (minimum 4 hours prior)  
 
B. After Payment Received  
• Reconfirm hotel and land arrangements with DMC immediately  
• Convert blocked services to confirmed status  
• Issue hotel & land vouchers within 48 hours of payment receipt  
• Issue flight tickets within 30 minutes of payment receipt (subject to fare validity)  
• Update CRM status to “Confirmed”  
• Send payment receipt to client  
 
C. Pre -Departure Process  
• Send final itinerary PDF 2 days prior to departure  
• Reconfirm all services with DMC 72 hours prior to arrival  
• Share emergency contact details (DMC + company)  
• Send visa checklist (if required)  
• Send travel tips & destination guidelines  
• Send travel insurance copy (if included)  
• Verify flight schedule changes 24 –48 hours prior
