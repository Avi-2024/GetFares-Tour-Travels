# Product Overview

## Project Identity
**Get2Vacation Travel CRM** - Enterprise-grade Customer Relationship Management system for tour and travel companies, built as a modular monolith with feature-based architecture.

## Purpose & Value Proposition
Centralized CRM platform that automates the complete travel sales lifecycle from lead capture to post-booking operations. Eliminates lead leakage, reduces response time, and provides real-time visibility into sales performance, revenue, and team productivity.

## Key Features & Capabilities

### Lead Management
- Automatic lead capture from Facebook, Instagram, Website, WhatsApp, Google Ads
- Smart lead distribution with rule-based routing (destination, budget, expertise)
- Round-robin and performance-based assignment
- Lead scoring (Hot/Warm/Cold) with anti-duplication logic
- 15-minute SLA tracking with escalation alerts
- Lead lifecycle tracking: Open → WIP → Quoted → Follow-up → Converted/Lost

### Quotation Management
- Beautiful branded quotation generation in 5-10 minutes
- Ready-made templates and custom package builder
- Auto-filling customer data with supplier cost tracking
- Automatic profit and margin calculation
- Multi-channel delivery (Email, WhatsApp PDF)
- Auto-reminders after 24 hours

### Booking & Operations
- End-to-end booking workflow with supplier coordination
- Payment tracking (minimum 50% advance, balance before D-2)
- Document management (passport, visa, tickets, hotel vouchers)
- Service confirmation blocking with deadline alerts
- Cancellation and refund workflow

### Visa Management
- Complete visa case pipeline: Document Collection → Submitted → Biometrics → Under Process → Approved/Rejected → Delivered
- Country-specific visa checklists
- Document upload and verification
- Appointment date tracking with expiry reminders
- Visa fees and supplier tracking

### WhatsApp Automation
- Automated customer communication sequences
- Triggers: New lead welcome, quotation sent, reminders, pre-travel, post-travel feedback
- Two-way WhatsApp chat integration
- Template messages with variables

### Sales Team Management
- Daily task dashboard with follow-up tracking
- Target vs Achievement monitoring
- Performance ranking and activity logs
- Workload balancing with auto-skip for agents on leave
- Manager alerts for unattended leads

### Customer Management
- Complete customer profiles with travel history
- Preference tracking for repeat sales
- Customer segmentation (Platinum/Gold/Silver/New)
- Special dates tracking (Anniversary, Birthday)
- Bulk email and WhatsApp campaigns

### Reports & Analytics
- Real-time dashboards: leads, conversions, revenue, profit
- Consultant-wise performance metrics
- Destination and lead source analytics
- Conversion funnel visualization
- Forecasting: pipeline revenue, seasonal trends
- Supplier performance tracking

### Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Admin: Full system access and configuration
- Managers: Team monitoring, target tracking, lead allocation oversight
- Sales Consultants: Lead management, quotations, status updates
- Visa Executives: Visa pipeline and document management
- Marketing: Campaign access, package performance, bulk communications
- Accounts: Payment updates, invoices, refund processing

### Package Management & Website Integration
- Package creation with SEO fields (meta title, description, keywords)
- "Publish to Website" toggle with auto-sync
- Auto-update website when prices change
- Auto-hide expired packages
- Lead capture from website enquiries with package tracking

## Target Users & Use Cases

### Primary Users
- **Tour & Travel Companies** managing holiday packages and visa services
- **B2C Travel Agencies** handling direct customer bookings
- **B2B Travel Operators** coordinating with other agencies
- **Visa Service Providers** managing worldwide visa applications

### Use Cases
1. **Lead-to-Booking Automation**: Capture leads from multiple channels, auto-assign to consultants, create quotations, track follow-ups, convert to bookings
2. **Team Performance Monitoring**: Track consultant activity, response times, conversion rates, and revenue generation
3. **Customer Lifecycle Management**: Maintain customer history, preferences, and automate repeat sales campaigns
4. **Visa Operations**: End-to-end visa case management with document tracking and appointment scheduling
5. **Financial Tracking**: Monitor payments, supplier costs, profit margins, and generate invoices
6. **Marketing Campaign Management**: Track lead sources, campaign ROI, and cost per lead
7. **Operational Efficiency**: Automate follow-ups, reminders, and customer communications via WhatsApp and email

## Business Impact
- Improve lead conversion ratio to 20-30%
- Achieve 15-minute lead response compliance
- Reduce manual follow-up errors
- Provide real-time revenue visibility
- Automate operational workflows
- Increase team accountability and transparency
